# MLAMH Scene SEO Optimization Loop

This document defines the operating loop for growing organic search visibility for MLAMH Scene without publishing random or duplicate content.

## Primary goals

1. Grow non-brand impressions for Saudi casting, acting, modeling, advertising, production, and talent searches.
2. Turn informational traffic into product discovery through contextual MLAMH CTAs.
3. Build topical authority around Talent, Publishers, Casting & Production, Stories, and Reports.
4. Avoid keyword cannibalization by assigning one primary search intent to one canonical article.

## Weekly review

For the previous 7 and 28 days, review Google Search Console for:

- queries with rising impressions
- pages with impressions but weak CTR
- queries ranking in positions 6–20
- pages losing clicks or impressions
- new queries not covered strongly by the current article
- multiple MLAMH URLs competing for the same query
- indexing status for newly published Scene articles

## Action rules

### High impressions + low CTR
Improve title and meta description before creating a new page.

### Position 6–20
Expand the existing article, strengthen internal links, and answer adjacent questions on the same intent.

### New long-tail query with different intent
Create a new article only when the searcher's goal is materially different from an existing page.

### Cannibalization
Choose the strongest canonical page, consolidate overlapping content, and link supporting pages toward it.

### Fresh official data
Update reports when newer Film Commission, Ministry of Culture, Vision 2030, or other authoritative Saudi sources are published. Keep the source link inside the article.

## Content clusters

### Talent
Pillars: starting acting/modeling, auditions, self-tape, portfolio/profile readiness.

### Publishers
Pillars: casting brief, finding actors/models, reviewing applicants, shortlist, commercial use cases.

### Casting & Production
Pillars: terminology, casting process, production roles, usage rights, call sheets, industry operations.

### Reports & Insights
Pillars: Saudi box office, local film performance, production incentives, creative-sector indicators, industry events.

## Internal linking rule

Every new article should link naturally to 2–4 relevant existing Scene articles and one appropriate product action when useful. Avoid repetitive exact-match anchor text.

## Technical checks

Monthly:

- sitemap contains all published Scene articles and category pages
- canonicals are self-referencing and localized
- Arabic/English hreflang pairs are present
- Article structured data is valid
- category pages expose CollectionPage, BreadcrumbList, and ItemList structured data
- no published Scene route returns 4xx/5xx
- no accidental noindex on public Scene routes

## Publishing cadence

Prefer 2–4 high-quality additions or updates per week over bulk low-value publishing. Search Console evidence should increasingly determine what gets written or refreshed next.
