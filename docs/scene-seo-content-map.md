# MLAMH Scene — SEO Content Map

Purpose: build organic discovery for MLAMH across Saudi searches related to acting, modeling, casting, production, advertising and talent sourcing. This is an editorial roadmap, not a publication queue. Every article should solve a real search intent, cite trustworthy sources when it contains factual market claims, and route the reader to a useful MLAMH action.

## Publishing rules

- Arabic first; English version when the topic has meaningful bilingual search or business value.
- One primary intent per page. Avoid publishing multiple pages that compete for the same query.
- Use natural Saudi terminology and known spelling variants where useful: كاستنج، كاستينج، كاستينغ.
- Market numbers must be sourced from official/public sources and clearly labeled as public-source analysis until MLAMH first-party data is representative.
- Practical guides should contain examples, checklists, related internal links, and a relevant CTA.
- Prefer durable evergreen topics, then refresh dated statistics annually.
- Do not fabricate interviews, quotes, salary figures, demand statistics or conversion claims.

## Recommended publishing sequence

### Wave 1 — capture direct demand
Publish 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17.

### Wave 2 — build topical authority
Publish 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20.

### Wave 3 — build Saudi market authority
Publish/refine 21–30, updating official numbers whenever new annual reports are released.

### Wave 4 — long-tail expansion
Publish 31–48 based on Search Console impressions, on-site search terms, user support questions and publisher briefs.

## Internal-linking model

- Talent guides → `/talent`, opportunities, related talent guides.
- Publisher/commercial guides → talent directory + request/brief flow.
- Reports → related reports + industry explainers + relevant product action.
- Glossary/explainers → at least one practical guide and one product page.
- City pages → talent directory filtered/positioned for that geography where supported.

## Measurement

Track monthly:
- non-brand organic impressions and clicks;
- queries containing casting/acting/model/talent terms;
- indexed Scene URLs;
- top 20 landing pages from search;
- organic visitors who create a talent account or start a publisher request;
- brand-query growth for ملامح / MLAMH;
- content gaps surfaced by Search Console and Scene search.

Do not judge the program only by immediate registrations. Early signals are impressions, indexed keyword coverage, backlinks/citations and growth in branded search.
