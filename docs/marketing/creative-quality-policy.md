# MLAMH Marketing Creative & Copy Quality Policy

1. External marketing copy must not use MLAMH's current talent count as social proof. Live supply counts remain internal analytics until leadership explicitly approves a scale claim for external use.
2. Human-facing outreach and client replies must remain editable before approval. Escaped line breaks must be normalized before display or delivery.
3. Instagram publishing always requires a publishable visual asset. Facebook visual formats (reel, story, carousel, video) require a publishable visual asset as well.
4. Content strategy creates copy drafts first, then a creative-production task. Social publishing approval is created only after a branded creative asset is materialized and attached.
5. Buffer execution remains approval-gated and channel-scoped. A ready/approved creative without an attached asset cannot be published.
6. External copy is validated again at channel execution, so weak live-supply count claims are blocked even if they entered the queue earlier.
