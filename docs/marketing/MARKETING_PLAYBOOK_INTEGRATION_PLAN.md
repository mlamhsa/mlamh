# MLAMH Marketing Playbook Integration Plan

Status: APPROVED BASELINE
Objective: Convert useful Marketing Skills knowledge into MLAMH-native playbooks without copying an external repository into the product.

## Principle
Marketing Skills provide frameworks and checklists. MLAMH systems remain the source of truth for execution, approvals, data, and external actions.

## Core playbooks to maintain inside MLAMH Knowledge
1. Product Positioning & Audience Context
2. Publisher Prospecting
3. Governed Cold Outreach
4. Email Follow-up & Reply Handling
5. Content Strategy & Brand Voice
6. Signup Conversion Review
7. Talent Onboarding Activation
8. Referral & Sharing Loops
9. Analytics & Attribution
10. Experiment Design / A-B Testing
11. SEO Audit
12. Programmatic SEO Guardrails
13. Multi-Country SEO / Site Architecture
14. Competitor Intelligence
15. Customer Research

## Playbook contract
Every playbook should contain:
- purpose,
- audience or workflow scope,
- inputs,
- decision checklist,
- prohibited actions,
- approval level,
- measurable outcome,
- telemetry/events to inspect,
- version and status.

## Execution safety
- A playbook may recommend or draft an action.
- Approval policy determines whether the action can execute.
- No playbook can create a parallel send path around existing email/social integrations.
- No playbook can expose protected talent information.
- No playbook can make commercial commitments or market activation decisions without authorized approval.

## Learning loop
After a campaign, outreach sequence, funnel experiment, or content cycle:
1. Capture outcome metrics.
2. Separate signal from low-volume noise.
3. Record the learning.
4. Update the relevant playbook only when evidence justifies a change.
5. Keep version history so a bad optimization can be reversed.
